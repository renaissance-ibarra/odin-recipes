Hamburger

Description

A classic juicy hamburger made with a seasoned beef patty, fresh vegetables, cheese, and a soft toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices of cheese
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

  1. Divide the ground beef into four equal portions.
  2. Shape each portion into a patty slightly wider than the hamburger buns.
  3. Season both sides of the patties with salt and black pepper.
  4. Heat a skillet or grill over medium-high heat and add the cooking oil.
  5. Cook the beef patties for about 4–5 minutes on one side.
  6. Flip the patties and cook for another 3–4 minutes, or until cooked to your preferred doneness.
  7. Place a slice of cheese on each patty during the final minute of cooking and allow it to melt.
  8. Toast the hamburger buns cut-side down in the same pan or on the grill until lightly golden.
  9. Spread mayonnaise, ketchup, and mustard on the buns, then layer the lettuce, tomato, onion, and cooked beef patty.
  10. Place the top bun over the fillings, gently press the hamburger together, and serve immediately.
