Pork Sisig

Description

A Filipino favorite made with crispy, chopped pork, sautéed onions, chili, and a savory-citrusy seasoning. It is traditionally served sizzling hot and pairs perfectly with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork belly
  • 200 g pork liver
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2–3 red or green chilies, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp calamansi juice
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 egg (optional)

Steps

  1. Rinse the pork belly and pork liver, then pat them dry with paper towels.
  2. Season the pork belly and liver with salt and black pepper.
  3. Grill, broil, or pan-sear the pork belly until browned and crispy on the outside and fully cooked inside.
  4. Cook the pork liver until completely done, then set it aside to cool slightly.
  5. Finely chop the cooked pork belly and pork liver into small pieces.
  6. Heat cooking oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant and slightly softened.
  7. Add the chopped pork and liver, then stir-fry for several minutes until the mixture becomes hot and slightly crispy.
  8. Add the soy sauce, calamansi juice, and chopped chilies, then mix thoroughly and adjust the seasoning to taste.
  9. Stir in the mayonnaise and cook for another minute, or transfer the mixture to a heated sizzling plate and top with an egg if desired.
  10. Serve the sisig immediately while sizzling, with extra calamansi and chilies on the side.
